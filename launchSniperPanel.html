Paper Launch Watcher

Separate account · early-pair simulation · no live execution
Paper only. This watcher discovers candidates from DexScreener every 30 seconds and refreshes open-position prices every 5 seconds, after a new pair is already visible to that feed. It is not an on-chain, first-block sniper and does not connect a wallet. It paper-buys $10 at a time, holds up to three positions, requires a RugCheck-reported LP lock of at least 70%, pair age ≤30 minutes, liquidity ≥$20,000, 24h volume ≥$25,000, 5m volume ≥$1,500, at least 12 five-minute trades, and ≥55% buys. Fees and price impact are modeled estimates. These filters cannot rule out rugs or guarantee fills.
